About the Story

Harry Potter, the boy who lived, begins his extraordinary journey as a young wizard discovering the secrets of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. The series follows his growth from a shy student into a brave leader who must confront the dark wizard Lord Voldemort and protect the people he loves.

The books and films blend wonder, danger, and heartfelt moments. They explore friendship, sacrifice, loyalty, and the enduring power of choosing what is right even when it is difficult.

  • Books: 7 main novels by J.K. Rowling
  • Films: 8 magical adventures on screen
  • School: Hogwarts, home of Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, and Slytherin
  • Magic: Spells, creatures, potions, and ancient mysteries
